Temple of Minerva

This Temple of Minerva was also built by Domitian. The temple was a hub for the Quinquatria, a five-day festival held to celebrate Minerva, hosting artisans and craftsmen, scholars and students, and ceremonial military arms.

Divorum

The Divorum was originally built to honour previous deified Roman emperors. It was built by Domitian to honour his father and brother, Vespasian and Titus.

Minucia Frumentaria

The Minucia Frumentaria served as a large grain distribution center. It completed administrative and logistical duties for the free or subsidised distribution of grain to Roman citizens.

Temple of Nymphs

Nymphs were deities associated with water, nature, and fertility. The temple served to 'spiritually protect' and give a sense of sacred order to the bread distribution operations.

Theatre of Balbus

The Theatre of Balbus was a place for performances, and provided a public space for cultural and social gatherings. Much like many other public buildings, it was a monument to glorify a wealthy Roman Elite, Lucius Cornelius Balbus the Younger.
