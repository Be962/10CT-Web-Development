Gardens of Agrippa

The Gardens of Agrippa were built to provide a public area for relaxation, discussion, and exercise. They held art, shaded groves, and a large artificial pool (stagnum). Agrippa built many monuments, helping gain public support for his ally Augustus Caesar.

Baths of Agrippa

The baths of Agrippa were originally built as a private estate, but were later gifted to the Roman public for free use after the death of Marcus Vipsanius Agrippa the owner.

Basilica of Neptune

The Basilica of Neptune was built to honour Neptune, and to celebrate the naval victories of Marcus Vipsanius Agrippa, built around 25 BC. It served as a public civic hall, tied to the surrounding imperial complex.

Baths of Alexander Severus

The baths of Alexander Severus were originally built by Emperor Nero. They were damaged by a fire, and restored and enlarged by Alexander Severus around 227-229 AD.
