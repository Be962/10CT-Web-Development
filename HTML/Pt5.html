Largo di Torres Argentina

The Largo di Torres Argentina served as a sacred precinct and political hub in ancient Rome. It contained 4 republican temples, adjacent to the Theatre of Pompey. It witnessed the assassination of Julius Caesar on the Ides of March.

Temple of Juturna

The Temple of Juturna was dedicated to Juturna, the goddess of fountains, wells, and springs. It was built around mid 3rd century BC, by Gaius Lutatius Catulus to celebrate his naval victory in the First Punic War.

Temple of Fortuna Huiusce Diei

The Temple of Fortuna Huiusce Diei represented luck on this exact day. It was known for having a circular floor plan, and was created by the Roman consul Quintus Lutatius Catalus

Temple of Feronia

The Temple of Feronia was dedicated to Feronia, the Italic goddess of fertility, health, and nature. It functioned as a social centre associated with the emancipation of slaves.

Temple of the Lares Permarini

The Temple of the Lares Permarini was the largest temple in the Largo de Torres Argentina. The Lares were general guardian deities, and the Lares Permarini were supposed to protect the Roman crews when they were away from Rome at sea.
