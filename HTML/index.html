Colosseum

Built by Vespasian and Titus from 72-82 AD, the Colosseum was the centre of showmanship for Rome. It hosted 50,000 to 80,000 spectators for public spectacles such as gladiatorial contests, public executions, and animal hunts.

Colossus Solis

Originally dedicated to Emperor Nero, after his death Vespasian added a crown and renamed it the Colossus Solis, after the sun god Sol.

Arch of Constantine

The Arch of Constantine was a triumphal arch dedicated to Constantine the Great, specifically to recognise Constantine's victory over Maxentius at the Battle at the Milvian Bridge in 312 AD
